## Materials and support

| Material role | Material | Slicer filament | G-code tool |
|---|---|---:|---|
| model | TINMORRY PETG-GF | 1 | T0 |
| interface | Bambu PLA Basic | 2 | T1 |

GF forms the model and support body; PLA forms the dense top contact. 3 PLA contact layers, 0 mm top gap and 0 mm contact spacing are configured. A preview showing one selected layer is not a count of the complete interface stack. Material assignment is also checked against positive extrusion under the actual T0/T1 commands.

Purge is 560 mm³ in each direction. Flushing: objects disabled, infill disabled, supports disabled. PLA flushing is limited to 12 mm³/s, independently of PLA's printing flow limit. Bottom contact retains 3 layers with a 0.18 mm gap and 0.5 mm spacing; it is a recorded exception, not the same finish as top contact.

The [reported Bambu workaround](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/11893#issuecomment-5575014503) concerns nozzle variant/mapping inconsistencies. Here all four per-filament maps are completed together, High Flow is pinned, and purge dimensions match the loaded filament count. Merely changing the variant fields was insufficient in our reproduction. Native export XML newlines are encoded so GUI reopening retains magnet pauses.

The Tinmorry recipe is frozen in `vendor/TINMORRY/resolved_P2S_06HF_PETG_GF.json` and hash-checked. The GUI user preset can change without silently changing this recipe.

User-requested calibration after a PETG-GF to PLA blockage: double the previous 280 mm3 purge in both directions and limit PLA flushing to 12 mm3/s. Printing flow limits and temperatures are unchanged. Physical changeover test pending. Actual native changeover requests are checked by `scripts/audit_changeover.py`.

## Inserts and screws

The V4 now uses 6 M3×8 socket-head screws, Ø3.4 mm retainer clearance holes and Ø4.6 mm × 4 mm blind insert bores, matching the shared M3 bore convention. The screw circle is Ø50.2 mm. The new retainer is Ø55.9 mm and passes through the unchanged Ø56.4 mm cap entry. The driver-side insert ligament is 1.60 mm; the retainer clearance-hole edge ligament is 1.15 mm. The cap, O-ring and driver seating surfaces are retained. Use the new body and retainers together; the imported M2 retention coupon is historical and does not qualify this M3 revision.

Use the existing **Hanglife HLTI-M3-001 — M3 x 5 x 4** inserts: M3 internal thread, Ø5 mm outside and 4 mm long. The Ø4.6 mm value describes our printed pilot, not the metal insert diameter. It leaves 0.4 mm nominal diametral interference for heat-setting. This records the established project bore convention; the supplied package drawing specifies the insert dimensions. The 4 mm inserts match the existing 4 mm-deep bores. The M3×8 screw engages 3.7 mm and stops 0.3 mm before the blind floor; 8 mm is the screw length under its head. The actual Ø5 mm insert envelope leaves 1.40 mm to the V4 driver cavity. [User-supplied package drawing](../vendor/HANGLIFE/M3x5x4_reference.png).

Regular LM M5 stepped insert bores, M3 driver/joint bores, and M2 auxiliary ties remain their own functional interfaces. Through holes and thread-forming pilots are not insert pockets. See [the insert inventory](INSERT_CATALOG.md) for those separate counts.

There is no universal solid-infill modifier around every insert or magnet. Structural LM/UM regions use the densities above; a 6-wall shell still applies in the 15% tweeter region and 10% wings. The no-floor bridge-root 100% modifier is retained as an explicit structural safeguard.

## Magnets

| Location | Disc | Grade | Selection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular Obi-Wan and preserved LM connections | Ø5 × 2 mm | N52 | Superimanes D-05-02-N52 |
| Crescent UM and matching upper wing connections; eight discs per assembly | Ø6 × 3 mm | N45 | Superimanes D-06-03 |

**The magnets are not all the same model.** The preserved LM connections remain D5×2 N52. The crescent UM/upper-wing pairs use D6×3 N45: four in the body and two in each selected upper wing. Do not substitute one size into the other pocket. Both are alignment/retention features with zero structural load credit.

D5 uses the shared qualified roof/skin geometry and single-traversal toolpath checks. D6 uses inclined, buried pockets and a swept loading-path check. The new D6 gate samples every cavity layer at ≤0.06 mm boundary spacing and verifies actual model beads, width bounds and insertion timing. In the seated-disc working region, the cavity-facing wall must have 1 connected footprint and at least 50% footprint overlap with the prior model layer. These are digital screening criteria, not bond-strength measurements. A same-geometry crop coupon is provided separately. Digital passage does not establish physical extrusion, pause bonding, insertion or retention; those remain pending a coupon print.

## Exceptions and blocker review

| Exception | Scope | Reason / required check |
|---|---|---|
| `regular_wings_no_support` | regular_wing | Existing print pose and D5 roof contract; no PLA is loaded or required. Fail if support extrusion exists. |
| `crescent_wings_supported` | crescent_wing | Curved replacement wings have different geometry. GF supports with PLA contact; cavity blockers must exclude trapped support. |
| `caps_full_ceiling` | crescent_accessories | Disable critical-only and small-overhang removal so the entire 44.6 mm cap ceiling receives three full PLA contact layers. |
| `tweeter_lightweight` | crescent_body | Nonprinting modifier above installed Y=421 mm overrides UM 100% with tweeter 15% gyroid. |
| `bottom_contact_legacy` | supported | Bottom contact retains 0.18 mm gap and 0.5 mm spacing. It is not equivalent to the dense zero-gap top contact; review if supports are allowed to start on model surfaces. |
| `bridge_root_solid` | no_floor_stand_lm_bottom | Local 100% bridge-root modifier remains as an explicit structural safeguard; redundant with current global 100%. |
| `insert_and_duct_blockers` | supported | Block only functional bore and route volumes. Check actual support bead intersection, not blocker count or colour. |
| `rear_T_bore_layer_boundary` | crescent_body | Report sub-half-layer PLA contact at the mouth separately from support inside the cylindrical bore; retain a numerical bound and witness. |
| `D6_physical_qualification` | crescent_D6 | Digital boundary, width and loading checks plus a same-geometry coupon are necessary; extrusion, insertion, pause bonding and retention require a physical print. |
| `service_entry_support_columns` | crescent_body | Block the complete projected cable entry until it leaves the enlarged M3 service chamber; a short fixed sample range allowed adjacent support to grow sideways into the bore. Actual duct clearance is a mandatory gate. |
| `auxiliary_bores_orientation` | regular_NL8_and_M2_ties | These holes lack dedicated bore blockers. NL8 opens away from the bed; ties rely on their enclosed position and support settings. Fresh slices are checked by scripts/audit_auxiliary_support.py. Recheck if orientation or support scope changes. |

The crescent body has 12 insert sites: four UM, two LM receivers and six tweeter M3 bores. Its support exclusions follow each complete bore to the blind floor; the obsolete narrow tip cavities are gone. Cable passages and magnet loading cavities are excluded separately. Native red blocker volumes are nonprinting tools, not physical plugs.

Remaining physical checks: insert installation, retaining-wall extrusion, magnet seating/polarity, bonding after each pause, PLA removal and cap/LM assembly fit. Acoustic performance is not qualified by mesh or slicer checks.
