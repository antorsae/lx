Juan LX521.4 MGNEO and BATMAN from the Sweetspot

2 Speakers - Rotation 0-90 deg, mic at the sweetspot

Juan's LX521.4 MGNEO and BATMAN, each the right speaker with the UM not inverted, measured on 13-Oct-2026 from the same listening seat, 20 minutes apart. The mic stayed at the seat, 114 cm high and 85 cm in front of the rear wall, while the speaker turned from 0 deg (baffle facing the wall) to 90 deg in 10 deg steps. Angles are speaker rotation, not off-axis angle: both speakers point at the seat near 45-50 deg, so 0 and 90 deg are each about 45 deg off-axis. Gated at 3 ms, before the rear-wall reflection (4.4-4.7 ms), with 1/12-octave smoothing, so the plots show what the speakers radiate toward the seat; Juan's ungated screenshots include the wall, which fills in the low rotations. The LX521.4 dips 10-14 dB at 5-7 kHz at every rotation; BATMAN does not near on-axis. The polar explorer is relative to the 0 deg rotation; there are no DI or beamwidth pages, since those take 0 deg as on-axis.
